# Brag Plan: Fernbrook dead space

## What is this app?
A web app that proves Fernbrook Furniture Co.'s "full" stores are mostly half-empty bins, lists the bin merges that free real space, and plans where every incoming delivery goes.

## The angle
"The stores are full" is the sentence everyone repeats and nobody checked. The video is a calm, dry, receipts-first correction: say the claim, show the bins, move six things, watch five bins come free, watch every delivery land. Fernbrook's own quiet brand (Made slowly. Made to stay.) is the delivery: no hype, just the plan working.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Giant Fraunces word "Full." on fern green. A beat later a small mono line types under it: "the stores, apparently". VO: "The stores are full. Apparently."

## Key moments (the middle)
- Four fullness cards fill one by one: Small 43%, Medium 49%, Large 48%, Bulk 57% (from the app's fullness page, `docs/pitch-evidence.md`). "2 empty bins" lands last.
- The merges list: Steel bracket set 22, B-02-2 to B-02-1, frees B-02-2; Bench leg, oak 14, B-01-2 to B-01-1; Garden bench, 2-seat 5, C-01-2 to C-01-1; Coach bolts M8 30, A-02-1 to A-01-3; Brass hinges 20, A-02-3 to A-02-2. Empty bins counter climbs 2 to 7.
- The deliveries list: PO-4417 Exterior varnish 1L x96 to A-05-1, WO-1031 Armrest pair, oak x48 to B-03-3, PO-4419 Stainless screws, WO-1033 Garden bench, 3-seat. Each gets a check. End state "6 of 6 placed, 3 bins spare".

## Outro / punchline
"No extra space needed." then the Fernbrook lockup and "Made slowly. Made to stay."

## User flow worth showing
1. Open the app: How full the stores are (percent bars, empty bins).
2. Merges page: what moves, and which bins it frees.
3. Deliveries page: every delivery placed, nothing moved twice.

## Tone
- Preset: deadpan
- Creative direction: a quiet, receipts-first correction in Fernbrook's own earthy brand voice
- Interpretation: long holds, plenty of linen space, one idea per scene, dry unhurried narrator; the numbers do the shouting.

## Format: landscape, 1920x1080
## Duration: 23 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: fern `#2C4535` (hook, outro) / linen `#F3EEE4` (app scenes)
- Accent: oak `#C9A074`; supporting moss `#8AA574`, brook `#5F8A94`, stone `#DDD5C6`
- Text: bark `#1F1B17` on linen; linen on fern
- Display font: Fraunces (weight 400, opsz 144, letter-spacing -0.03em)
- Body font: Instrument Sans; codes and numbers in DM Mono
- Strongest visual element: the fullness cards with progress bars, and the fern-frond logo mark (`public/brand/logo-mark-fern.svg`)

## Share copy (draft)
Everyone said the stores were full. We checked: most bins are half empty. Six moves free five bins and every delivery fits.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, sparse professional accents; music sits under the voice
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (~110 BPM)
- Music treatment: in at 0s low, ducked to ~0.13 while the narrator speaks, lifts slightly in the gaps, fades out over the last 2s
- Music cue guidance: preset `cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md`; beat spacing ~0.55s. Strong cues 15.82s (merges counter payoff) and 20.19s (logo landing)
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music energy gently warms the oak underline and logo glow
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched; soft clicks on bar fills and checkmarks, one drop as "Full." is struck through
- Audio-coupled moments: fullness cards arriving one by one; merge rows arriving one by one; delivery checks ticking
- Restraint rule: never louder than the narrator; no whooshes, no risers

## Voiceover script
Voice: Kokoro `af_heart`, unhurried. Lines complement the visuals, they do not read the screen.

1. "The stores are full. Apparently."
2. "Most bins are about half empty. And next week's deliveries still won't fit."
3. "So: six moves. Five bins come free."
4. "Every delivery gets a home. Three bins to spare."
5. "No extra space needed."

(About 38 words, roughly 15s of speech inside a 23s video.)

## Storyboard

### Scene 1: Hook, 4.0s
Fern background. "Full." in huge Fraunces linen, centre. At ~1.4s an oak line strikes under it; the mono caption "the stores, apparently" types beneath. Text settled ~2s.
Sequential/interaction: yes, the caption types out character by character.
Audio intent: dry, a little smug.
Audio-coupled idea: soft key ticks on the typing, one low drop when the line strikes.
Music: warm bed fades in.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2: The receipts, 5.5s
Linen background, app chrome (fern header, Fernbrook mark, "How full" active). Heading "How full the stores are". Four cards (Small 43%, Medium 49%, Large 48%, Bulk 57%) arrive one by one, bars filling. Then "2 empty bins" settles. Each card holds at least 1.2s.
Sequential/interaction: yes, four cards in sequence, about 1.1s apart so numbers stay readable.
Audio intent: quiet realisation.
Audio-coupled idea: a soft click per card as its bar fills.
Music: ducked under VO.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3: Six moves, 6.0s
Merges page. Heading "Merges: what moves, and which bins it frees". Five merge rows arrive one by one (product, qty, from → to, "frees B-02-2"). An "Empty bins" counter climbs 2 → 7. Rows stay on screen together after the last lands.
Sequential/interaction: yes, rows stack one by one, then the counter rolls up.
Audio intent: satisfying, orderly.
Audio-coupled idea: a click per row, a drop on the 7. Counter payoff beat-locked to strong cue 15.82s.
Music: bed, lifts slightly after VO.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4: Everyone gets a home, 4.5s
Deliveries page. Rows (PO-4417 Exterior varnish 1L x96 → A-05-1; WO-1031 Armrest pair, oak x48 → B-03-3; PO-4419 Stainless screws x120 → A-01-1, A-01-2; WO-1033 Garden bench, 3-seat x30 → C-02-1, C-01-2) each get a moss checkmark. End chip: "6 of 6 placed · 3 bins spare".
Sequential/interaction: yes, checkmarks tick down the list.
Audio intent: settled, relieved.
Audio-coupled idea: a tick per check.
Music: bed.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5: Outro, 3.0s
Back to fern. Fern-frond mark and wordmark. "No extra space needed." in Fraunces, then "Made slowly. Made to stay." in small oak caps. Music fades out. Logo landing beat-locked to strong cue 20.19s.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: quiet landing.
Music: fade out.

**Music mood for this video:** deadpan, warm
**Audio summary:** a low warm bed under a dry narrator, soft clicks on every arrival, one drop at the strike-through and one at the payoff, fading to silence on the logo.
